Context

One platform. Multiple products. One design language.

Solutions Workspace is Salesforce's centralised hub for Solution Engineers — combining official content, community resources, demo org tools, activity logging, and an AI-powered Solutions Agent in one interface. At 35,000+ users globally, it's one of the largest internal platforms at Salesforce.

What made this project unique as a designer: SW isn't one monolithic product. It's a custom design system — its own palette, typography, and component library — that has to hold together across several distinct sub-products. SE Insights, Activity Editor, and the core SW experience are each their own workstreams, but share the same visual and interaction language I was responsible for maintaining.

Sub-Projects

Three workstreams, one design system

Each sub-project had its own brief, timeline, and stakeholders — but shared the same design foundations. Here's how I approached each one.

01 SE Insights

SEs had no clear view of their own performance inside SW — how many hours logged, deal contributions, or whether they were on track. SE Insights brought that data to the surface with an in-app dashboard and contextual alerts, giving SEs a real-time picture of their quarter without leaving the platform.

SE Insights — dashboard design and Activity Editor overlay in Figma

02 Activity Editor — Confirming Activities A/B Test

The Activity Editor needed a rethink — SEs were unclear on how to log and confirm their activities. I ran a structured A/B test with two distinct prototypes: Option A focused on clarity and simplicity; Option B offered more layout flexibility. 9 SEs were interviewed through structured usability sessions.

Prototype Option A — Activity Editor with Suggested Activities tab

03 Solutions Workspace Core

Beyond sub-products, I contributed to the core SW experience — including the homepage redesign, opportunity selector, and the Modern Interface Refresh that brought the custom design system's updated palette and components to all main surfaces.

SW Core — Deals view and opportunity selector modal

Approach

Research-led. System-aware. Always shipped.

01
Design System First
Every new component or pattern was evaluated for system fit — does it extend what exists, or introduce debt? Consistency at 35k users requires discipline from day one.
02
Research Before Pixels
User interviews, in-app workshops, and structured A/B tests before committing to a direction. Assumptions were treated as hypotheses, not facts.
03
Iterative & Shipped
Unlike vision concepts, this work was live. Features had to be scoped to be buildable — balancing ideal UX with real engineering constraints and quarterly release cycles.
