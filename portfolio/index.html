Hey, I'm Pierre (pee-air bang-tuh)

I'm a Senior Experience Designer at Salesforce in Dublin — product-first, research-led, and a strong believer that great enterprise UX is just as important as consumer UX (it's just harder to pull off).

I started in visual and brand design at early-stage startups in Lyon and Paris 🇫🇷, then moved into enterprise UX at Salesforce, where I've spent 7+ years owning strategy and end-to-end design for complex seller platforms. I'm also an internal champion of AI-assisted design — I've piloted Gemini, Claude, and Cursor across team workflows and led offsite workshops on how to use them effectively.
