Q2C Deal Optimization

Salesforce sellers were spending hours wrestling with spreadsheets, approvals, and fragmented tools to close a single deal. We designed a next-generation proposal experience — end-to-end, agentic-powered, and legal-ready — from scratch.

Context

Closing enterprise deals is broken

At Salesforce, the Quote-to-Cash (Q2C) process is the critical path from "customer interested" to "contract signed." But for Account Executives, it had become a maze of manual steps, custom spreadsheets, fragmented approvals, and legal back-and-forth — before a single deal could close.

This project sat within Salesforce's internal Q2C Innovation initiative, working directly inside Org62 — Salesforce's own instance of Salesforce — at the Opportunity level. The goal: design a next-generation experience that sellers would actually want to use.

The Problem

Too much red tape to close deals

Sellers were starting from complex, multi-scenario proposals — different pricing configurations, discount levels, contract terms — and had to manually reconcile all of it before getting to a legal-ready contract. Each step introduced friction, delay, and risk of error.

How might we

"How might we help sellers start from a complex, multi-scenario proposal to create an optimally configured and legal-ready contract swiftly — in a way that increases customer trust and reduces attrition?"

Approach

Designing the future of work through rigorous UX practice

Rather than starting with wireframes, we started with the desired end-state: a legal-ready contract. Then we worked backward — identifying every data point, decision, and approval required to get there. This reverse-engineering approach kept us anchored to real seller outcomes, not assumptions.

The Solution

A unified proposal journey — powered by agentic automation

We designed the Deal Optimization experience as an app living inside Org62 at the Opportunity level — covering the complete end-to-end proposal journey in one place. The key shift: every manual task that previously required spreadsheets or back-and-forth with Deal Desk was replaced with intelligent, agentic automation.

Before After

Drag to compare — wireframe exploration vs. final AI-powered prototype

Sellers can now create and compare multiple pricing scenarios in parallel, see a real-time Deal Score (0–100) reflecting approval probability and margin impact, and get automatic Approval Routing Impact previews — all before submitting to Legal.

The experience was designed with Salesforce's Lightning Design System constraints while pushing for a significantly more capable and intuitive interface than sellers had ever had access to internally.

What's Next

From vision to MVP

This was a vision concept — intentionally designed to show what's possible before scoping down to what's buildable. The next phase was defined around four priorities:

  • 01 Test the solution with the target audience — validate the concept with real AEs in structured usability sessions.
  • 02 Define MVP and iterations based on effort-to-value — work with engineering to scope the highest-impact features for a first release.
  • 03 Define success metrics and governance — align on what "better" looks like: time-to-contract, approval cycle reduction, seller satisfaction.
  • 04 Identify the next area to dig into — use research learnings to prioritize which seller pain point to tackle next.
