Guided Solution Selling

Salesforce sellers were manually hunting for the right challenges, plays, and stories to close a deal. We designed an AI-powered recommendation engine that surfaces the right content at the right stage, instantly.

Context

Sellers shouldn't have to hunt for the right story

Guided Solution Selling is part of Salesforce's internal initiative to make sellers more effective at every deal stage. The pain was clear: Account Executives were spending valuable time manually searching for relevant customer challenges, sales plays, and success stories, a slow, error-prone process that eroded confidence and deal velocity.

This project lived inside Org62 (Salesforce's own instance of Salesforce) at the Opportunity level. The goal: replace manual configuration friction with intelligent, data-driven recommendations that adapt to each deal in real time.

The Problem

Manual configuration slows every deal

Sellers were starting each opportunity from scratch, manually selecting customer challenges, matching sales plays, and finding relevant success stories. No intelligent surface, no context-awareness, no time savings. Every field was a decision that slowed them down.

How might we

"How might we leverage intelligent, data-driven recommendations to instantly map customer challenges directly into an optimally configured, legally compliant contract layout, guaranteeing full visibility and swift execution?"

Manual
Every challenge, play, and story selected by hand, no intelligence, no context
3 layers
Challenges, Sales Plays, Success Stories, all disconnected from deal context
0→1
No existing recommendation layer in Org62, built entirely from scratch

Approach

Solution-first. Data-driven. Context-aware.

Rather than designing around existing UI patterns, we started from the seller's desired outcome: the right content, at the right deal stage, with the least friction. Then we mapped backward, identifying every signal (stage, industry, motion, persona) that should influence what gets surfaced.

01
Map the recommendation logic
Reverse-engineered the ideal output (a legally compliant, fully configured opportunity) and mapped what data inputs were needed to get there automatically.
02
Design for all modalities
Handled three distinct states: brand new opportunity (no data), partial information, and opportunity with changing stage, each requiring a different recommendation strategy.
03
Build & validate fast
Used AI Studio + Google Stitch to build a working demo significantly faster than traditional prototyping, enabling real concept validation with stakeholders.

The Solution

AI-powered recommendations, surfaced in context

We designed a Guided Selling modal that lives inside the Salesforce Opportunity page, showing recommended Challenges, Sales Plays, Success Stories, and Products filtered by industry, motion, persona, and region. Sellers can add the right content in seconds instead of minutes.

The experience adapts to deal context: a brand-new opportunity surfaces generic high-signal recommendations, while a later-stage opportunity with known challenges gets hyper-targeted plays. Stage changes trigger a smart re-recommendation prompt, keeping sellers aligned at every step.

What's Next

From vision to tested feature

This was a vision concept designed to demonstrate what intelligent, context-aware selling could look like inside Org62. The next phase was defined around three priorities:

  • 01 Validate with real sellers, run structured usability sessions with Account Executives to test recommendation relevance and modal interaction patterns.
  • 02 Scope the MVP, work with engineering to prioritize which recommendation types (Challenges first, or Plays?) and which deal stages to target in a first release.
  • 03 Define success metrics, align on what "better" looks like: reduction in manual data entry time, increase in recommendation adoption rate, deal velocity improvement.
